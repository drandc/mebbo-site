What is on the test

The 13 official chapters, in full.

The Swedish citizenship test draws on thirteen set topics, published by UHR and Skolverket in the free study material Sverige i fokus. Mebbo covers every one of them. The chapter titles below are the official Swedish ones, so you will recognise them wherever else you meet them.

01Landet Sverige (The country of Sweden)
02Sveriges demokratiska system (Sweden's democratic system)
03Så här styrs Sverige (How Sweden is governed)
04Politiska val och partier (Elections and political parties)
05Lag och rätt (Law and justice)
06Mediernas roll (The role of the media)
07Mänskliga rättigheter (Human rights)
08Arbetsmarknad och privatekonomi (Work and personal finance)
09Välfärdssamhället (The welfare society)
10Sveriges moderna historia (Sweden's modern history)
11Sverige och omvärlden (Sweden and the wider world)
12En sekulär stat och ett mångreligiöst land (A secular state, a multi-faith country)
13Traditioner och högtider (Traditions and holidays)

Every chapter in Mebbo is written in plain Swedish, with a built-in glossary for the harder civic vocabulary. The English translations above are here to orient you, not to study from. See the full Swedish site.
