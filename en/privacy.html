Privacy Policy

Effective from 1 September 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Mebbo app for mobile devices (the "Application"), created by André Crous (the "Service Provider") as a free, ad-free study aid for the Swedish citizenship civics test (medborgarskapsprovet). This policy explains what information the Application collects and how it is used.

No Account Required

The Application does not require you to create an account, and does not collect your name, email address, or any other personal identifier in order for you to use it. Your study progress, chapter completion, practice history, and test-date settings are stored locally on your device only, using the device's local storage. The Service Provider does not have access to this data and it is not transmitted anywhere.

If you uninstall the Application, or use the Application's built-in "clear history" function, this locally stored data is permanently deleted.

Information the Application Does Not Collect

To be explicit about what this Application does not do:

  • It does not require or ask for your email address, name, or any other personal identifier.
  • It does not use any analytics or tracking SDK.
  • It does not use any advertising SDK, and does not display advertisements.
  • It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, or any other device permission beyond what is needed for the Application to run.
  • It does not share, sell, or rent any information to third parties for marketing purposes.

Chapter Images

Every chapter lesson includes an illustration. All illustrations are bundled directly with the Application and load with no network request at all — no external image service is contacted, and no information about which chapter you are viewing is disclosed to any third party.

In-App Purchases

If you choose to make an in-app purchase to unlock additional practice content, the purchase is processed by RevenueCat, a third-party purchase-management service, working together with Google Play, the app store platform through which you downloaded the Application. RevenueCat and Google Play process transaction information (such as purchase confirmation, transaction ID, and purchase date) in order to verify and record your purchase. The Service Provider receives a record of which product was purchased and when, in order to grant you the correct access within the Application, but does not receive your payment card details, which are handled entirely by Google Play.

RevenueCat's software runs on an anonymous, device-generated identifier; the Application never sends it your name or email address. As with any third-party SDK, RevenueCat's software does transmit some device and app metadata (such as your device type and app version) to RevenueCat's own servers as part of its normal operation.

You can review RevenueCat's own privacy practices at revenuecat.com/privacy, and Google Play's at policies.google.com/privacy.

Data Deletion

Mebbo: Your study progress, chapter completion, practice history, and test-date settings are stored only on your own device. To delete this data, open Mebbo, go to the Jag (Me) tab, and use the "clear history" function, which deletes it immediately and directly on your device with no need to contact anyone. Uninstalling the Application also deletes this data.

Purchase records: If you have made an in-app purchase, a record of that transaction (product purchased, transaction ID, purchase date) is held by RevenueCat and Google Play, not by the Service Provider directly. To request deletion of this data, contact RevenueCat or Google Play directly using the links below, or contact the Service Provider at andrejohancrous@gmail.com and the Service Provider will pass on a deletion request on your behalf. Deleting this data does not affect entitlement to a purchase already made; it only concerns the underlying transaction record.

  • RevenueCat: revenuecat.com/privacy
  • Google Play: support.google.com/googleplay

Data Retention

Because the Application does not collect personal data on its own servers, there is no server-side account data for the Service Provider to retain or delete on your behalf. Your local study data remains on your device until you delete it yourself, uninstall the Application, or use the in-app "clear history" function. Purchase records related to any in-app purchase are retained by RevenueCat and Google Play in accordance with their own retention practices, referenced above.

Children's Privacy

The Application is not directed at children under the age of 16, consistent with the age requirement in the Terms and Conditions. The Service Provider does not knowingly collect any information from children under 16. Since the Application does not collect personal data in the first place, there is no personal data of any user, including children, held on the Service Provider's own systems.

Security

The Service Provider values your trust in providing any information through the Application and takes reasonable steps to protect it. Because study progress is stored locally on your own device rather than on a server operated by the Service Provider, the security of that data also depends on the security of your own device.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

The Service Provider may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Material changes will be reflected by posting the updated policy here with a new effective date.

This Privacy Policy is effective as of 1 September 2026.

Your Consent

By using the Application, you consent to the processing of information as described in this Privacy Policy.
